All in Excel. One-by-one Kyriba entry. Monthly reports by hand.

Netting

Manual Kyriba transcription. Forecast blind to netting until after settlement.

Investment

80% in Kyriba. 20% manual. Compliance checked after execution.

Chapter 5

One Pattern, Many Domains

Every module you just explored runs on the same architecture. The repetition is deliberate — the pattern is the argument.

Six Principles

Cloud as Universal Adapter

File drops on shared storage. No IT dependency to start.

Email or API — Your Choice

Use M365 + Power Automate, REST APIs, or SFTP — pick whichever fits the vendor and use case.

Excel or Database — Your Choice

Start with Excel for speed; graduate to SQL when scale demands it. AI wraps either one.

System-of-Record Respect

Kyriba, CoreProcess, ERP stay authorities. AI fills gaps.

Human-in-the-Loop

AI prepares, humans decide. Approval chains unchanged.

Source-Side Standardisation

Push upstream systems to output clean data.
